Approach

How a project actually runs.

01

Understand the audience

Before any content plan, I look at who you're actually reaching now and who you should be reaching, using research methods rather than guesswork.

02

Set content pillars

Three to five recurring themes that give your page a personality people recognize, instead of a feed that reads different every week.

03

Write and plan

Captions, calendars, and formats mapped out in advance, so posting stays consistent even in busy weeks.

04

Review and adjust

Regular check-ins on what's landing and what isn't, with the plan updated rather than left on autopilot.
